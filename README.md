# Deven Kashyap

**Senior Scrum Master · Agile delivery · Team enablement**

I help teams make complex work easier to plan, discuss and verify. My focus is on clearer ownership, practical ways of working, useful delivery reporting, and making risks visible early enough to act.

## How I work

- **Create clarity:** establish a shared view of priorities, dependencies and decisions.
- **Enable the team:** facilitate planning, reviews and continuous improvement without claiming other people's implementation work.
- **Make evidence useful:** connect status updates to verifiable sources and distinguish a plan, a completed action and an outcome.

## Review package (access-controlled)

I maintain four separate materials for authorised reviewers:

1. **Contribution story — interactive HTML:** my role, actions, observed results and supporting evidence.
2. **Contribution presentation — PowerPoint:** a concise manager discussion.
3. **Sprint report — interactive HTML:** delivery views across configured sprint windows.
4. **Sprint presentation — PowerPoint:** a concise leadership review of the same windows.
